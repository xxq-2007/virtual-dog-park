Dog Encyclopedia

Meet our three residents. Each card shares the basics: size, personality, and everyday care.

Beagle

Size
Small to medium: about 33-40 cm tall and 9-14 kg. Compact, sturdy, and solid.
Personality
Merry, curious, and friendly. Beagles love company and follow their noses wherever they lead.
Everyday care
Daily walks with plenty of sniffing time, careful portion control because they love food, and regular ear cleaning.

A Beagle's nose has roughly 220 million scent receptors - one of the best in the dog world!

Golden Retriever

Size
Large: about 51-61 cm tall and 25-34 kg. Strong, athletic, and well built.
Personality
Gentle, intelligent, and eager to please. Famous for being wonderful with families and children.
Everyday care
Plenty of exercise such as walks, swimming, and fetch; brush the coat a few times a week; check the ears after swimming.

Golden Retrievers were first bred in Scotland to fetch shot waterfowl for hunters - water is still their happy place.

Poodle

Size
Comes in three sizes: Standard (over 38 cm), Miniature, and Toy - so there is a Poodle for almost any home.
Personality
Proud, active, and one of the smartest breeds around. Poodles love learning tricks and showing off.
Everyday care
Needs professional clipping every 4-6 weeks and regular brushing to prevent mats, plus daily exercise and puzzle games.

The fancy Poodle haircut started as a practical hunting trim: pom-poms once protected joints in cold water.
